3. Information Collected by the Application

NITYAM WORK collects information strictly necessary to provide habit building, routine planning, discipline tracking, and optional cloud backup features. Information collected falls into two primary categories:

  1. User-Provided Information: Data you author, configure, or submit within the app.
  2. Automatically Collected Telemetry & System State: Technical identifiers and device parameters required for app reliability, reminder scheduling, and ad delivery.

4. Information Users Provide Voluntarily

  • Profile Data: Name, chosen display username, avatar emoji, personal bio or motto, user role (e.g., Student, Professional), and daily habit target goals.
  • Authentication Credentials: Email address and hashed password when creating an email account, or verified identity tokens from Google Sign-In or Apple ID.
  • Habit Definitions & Logs: Habit names (e.g., "Morning Run", "Deep Work"), schedule frequencies, target quantities, custom tags, daily completion check-in times, streak records, and mood/reflection journal notes.
  • Focus Timer Records: Duration of completed focus sessions (e.g., 25-minute Pomodoro intervals) and associated habit categories.
  • User Support Correspondence: Feedback, bug reports, or support questions you send to our email address.

5. Automatically Collected Information & Device Information

When you use the Application, certain diagnostic and device parameters may be processed automatically to ensure operational stability:

  • Device Model & Manufacturer: To adapt responsive UI layouts and verify hardware alarm compatibility.
  • Operating System Version: Android OS version (e.g., Android 14/15/16) to determine available notification and exact alarm APIs.
  • Network State: Whether an active Wi-Fi or cellular data connection exists, preventing battery drain from futile cloud synchronization attempts when offline.
  • Session Timestamps: Local timestamps used to calculate habit streak continuity across time zones.

6. Permissions & Their Purposes

The Application requests only the minimal set of Android system permissions required to operate its features:

Permission Classification Explicit Purpose
android.permission.INTERNET Normal / Install-Time Enables optional cloud synchronization with Firebase Firestore, Firebase Authentication, Google Gemini AI habit coaching requests, and ad delivery.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Normal / Install-Time Allows checking whether the device is connected to the internet before initiating network operations.
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS Runtime (Android 13+) Allows the app to display scheduled habit reminders, streak alerts, morning briefs, and evening reflections requested by the user.
android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM & USE_EXACT_ALARM Special / System Allows precise triggering of user-configured habit reminder alarms and focus timer interval chimes.
android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Normal / Install-Time Restores and reschedules your active daily habit alarms and notifications after your device restarts.
android.permission.VIBRATE Normal / Install-Time Provides tactile haptic feedback when completing a habit check-in or when a focus timer interval ends.

Zero-Permission Storage: NITYAM WORK does not request broad media or external storage permissions (such as READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE).

7. How We Use Collected Information

  • Core Service Delivery: Tracking daily habit completions, computing streak counts, displaying completion calendars, and managing focus countdowns.
  • Notifications: Triggering alarms and notification banners for user-scheduled routines.
  • Authentication & Security: Verifying user identity via email link verification, Google Sign-In, or Apple ID, and securing cloud-synced habit records against unauthorized access.
  • AI Habit Coaching: Formulating personalized habit advice and streak tips through the Google Gemini API using aggregated, non-sensitive habit statistics (completion rates and category names).
  • Advertising: Serving contextual and relevant advertisements to support free access to the app.

8. Data Storage, Security & Retention

Local Storage (Room SQLite Database)

Your habit definitions, check-in history, reflection notes, and preferences are stored in a private SQLite database inside the app's sandboxed internal storage directory. Other applications on the device cannot read this database.

Cloud Storage (Firebase Firestore)

If you sign in, your data is synced with Google Cloud Firebase Firestore under strict user-scoped security rules. Data in transit is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3), and data at rest is encrypted using AES-256 by Google Cloud infrastructure.

Data Retention

Your personal data is retained for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide you with habit tracking history. If you delete your account or uninstall the app in guest mode, your data is erased as described in Section 16.

9. Third-Party Services & SDKs

11. Advertising Identifiers & User Privacy Controls

On Android devices, you possess direct control over how advertising identifiers are used:

  • Resetting Your Advertising ID: You can reset your device's GAID at any time, unlinking prior ad history from future interactions. Open Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID.
  • Deleting Your Advertising ID: In modern Android versions (Android 12+), you can delete your GAID entirely by navigating to Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID. When deleted, ad networks receive a string of zeros in place of an identifier.
  • Personalized vs. Non-Personalized Ads: Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g., EEA, UK, California) and consent settings, you may receive non-personalized ads, which are served based on contextual app content rather than your browsing history across other apps.
  • Google Ad Center: You can customize your Google advertising preferences online at https://myadcenter.google.com/.

16. Account Deletion & Data Deletion Procedures

In accordance with Google Play Store User Data Policy requirements, NITYAM WORK provides complete self-service data and account deletion pathways:

Method 1: In-App Direct Account Deletion

  1. Open NITYAM WORK on your Android device.
  2. Navigate to the Settings tab.
  3. Select Account, then tap Delete Account.
  4. Review the warning prompt, type DELETE to confirm, and tap Permanently Delete Account.

This immediately and permanently destroys your Firebase Authentication profile, removes your synced Firestore document, and wipes local Room database logs.

Method 2: Email Data Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the application or cannot access your device, you may submit a formal data deletion request by emailing mahesh.akbari@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request - NITYAM WORK". Please provide your registered account email. We will process and permanently purge your records within thirty (30) days and confirm completion via email.
